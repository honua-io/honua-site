The 2026.1 operating envelope

← Back to operations

Status: 2026.1 Release Candidate (2026.1-rc.1), published 2026-10-30. Server image: ghcr.io/honua-io/honua-server:2026.1-rc. GA qualification is the commitment in the release notes, not a completed qualification. This page records operating boundaries.

Source: honua-release docs/2026.1-operating-envelope.md at abf1f40c23a9d3979f443c798a05d00f6c748fb7. Historical measurements in that document are not the RC pin.

2026.1 tiers

  • GA targets: Docker Compose on a single host, and AWS ECS. Qualification of those targets is still pending.
  • Preview: AWS Lambda, Helm on EKS and other Kubernetes distributions, the GP catalog, and Honua Studio. Preview surfaces are in the RC. Behaviour may change before GA.
  • Architecture: x86_64 only.
  • Tenancy: single-tenant only. Multi-tenancy is internal and is not a customer deployment.

2026.1 ships with licensing disabled (Licensing__Mode=Disabled).
