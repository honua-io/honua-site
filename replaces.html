Three facts that hold across every row.

Self-hosted, in your cloud, on your network.

Honua runs in your AWS or Azure account, on your Kubernetes, or on a single host. Honua does not offer a hosted service. Source is available for the server and Apache-2.0 for the SDKs, Console, and Studio.

Where it runsHow the editions and licenses work

One image instead of an assembled stack.

An assembled stack is many projects, each with its own container, configuration, upgrade cadence, and authentication story. Honua Server is one deployment with one capability manifest, one admin surface, and one set of credentials.
