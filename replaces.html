// the difference, stated once

Three facts that hold across every row.

VS SAAS
Self-hosted
your cloud, your data, your network
  • Honua runs in your AWS or Azure account, on your Kubernetes, or on a single host. Mapbox, CARTO, and Felt are hosted services.
  • Source is available for the server and Apache-2.0 for the SDKs, Console, and Studio.
How the editions and licenses work →
VS OPEN SOURCE
One image
instead of an assembled stack
  • The open-source column is many projects, each with its own container, configuration, upgrade cadence, and authentication story.
  • Honua Server is one deployment with one capability manifest, one admin surface, and one set of credentials.
Dated benchmark against GeoServer →
VS ARCGIS
Flat annual capacity pricing
no named-user seats, request credits, or data-volume billing
  • Price follows serving capacity, not head count. Analysts, field crews, and public viewers do not change the bill.
  • Your existing ArcGIS clients keep working against Honua Server while you decide what else to move.
