What Honua replaces.

Your desktop GIS clients and QGIS keep working against Honua Server through GeoServices REST and OGC. Applications built on vendor SDKs move to the Honua SDKs: codemods and AI port your JavaScript, Python and .NET code.

Three facts that hold across every row.

Self-hosted: your cloud, your data, your network.

Honua runs in your AWS or Azure account, on your Kubernetes, or on a single host. Mapbox, CARTO, and Felt are hosted services. Source is available for the server and Apache-2.0 for the SDKs, Console, and Studio.

Against SaaSHow the editions and licenses work

One image instead of an assembled stack.

The open-source column is many projects, each with its own container, configuration, upgrade cadence, and authentication story. Honua Server is one deployment with one capability manifest, one admin surface, and one set of credentials.

Against open sourceDated benchmark against GeoServer

Flat annual capacity pricing.

No named-user seats, request credits, or data-volume billing. Price follows serving capacity, not head count. Analysts, field crews, and public viewers do not change the bill. Your desktop GIS clients keep working against Honua Server through GeoServices REST while you decide what else to move.
