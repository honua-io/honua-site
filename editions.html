THE COMMERCIAL DIRECTION

Scale with the work.
Not the headcount.

We are developing usage-based metering, capacity bands, or a combination. The pricing should support elastic workloads—not penalize distributing the same work across smaller instances.

For customer-hosted deployments, the Honua software fee is separate from infrastructure, model-provider charges, and optional support or services.

No speculative rate card. Bring a representative workload and we’ll agree evaluation scope and commercial terms before work begins.
