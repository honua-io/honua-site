THE COMMERCIAL DIRECTION

Scale with the work.
Not the headcount.

We’re working out usage-based metering, capacity bands, or a combination. Splitting the same work across smaller instances should not create a pricing penalty.

For customer-hosted deployments, the Honua software fee is separate from infrastructure, model-provider charges, and optional support or services.

We haven’t finalized the rates. Bring a representative workload, and we’ll agree on the evaluation scope and commercial terms before work begins.
