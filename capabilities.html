Every capability. One evidence trail.

← Back to claims & evidence

Pricing, claims, demos, and samples each show a slice of what Honua does. This page joins them: one row per capability, with its edition, current status, evidence, documented gaps, and links to see it live, try it, and read the full evidence page. Tick the capabilities you use today to build a shareable, filtered view and a rough edition/capacity estimate.

What this table establishes. It is generated from capabilities.v1.json, a draft fixture checked into this repository ahead of the real evidence artifact. It will be replaced by the capability-matrix.v1.json published by honua-server CI (honua-io/honua-server#2892 ↗). Counts shown here are either already published on claims.html / the compatibility matrix, or explicitly marked as not yet published — none are invented.

How to read a row

Source evaluation
Runnable and inspectable from public server trunk today. A numbered variant carries a dated CITE or conformance count.
Partial coverage
Documented, operation-level coverage and gaps exist, but no aggregate test count is published for this exact capability yet.
Preview
Implemented and disclosed, but pre-GA: disabled by default, opt-in only, and may change before general availability.
Proof pending
No public evidence artifact is published for this exact capability yet.
Edition chips
Community (free, self-host, ELv2), Pro, and Enterprise — see pricing for what each adds.
Gaps in-row
Every row states what the capability does not yet cover, in the row itself — not on a separate page you have to find.
