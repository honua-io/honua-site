// status vocabulary

What the labels mean.

Source-backed
Shipped, with linked evidence for the exact scope — check it yourself.
Preview partial
Real and usable for evaluation today. Coverage is partial and the gaps are documented, not hidden.
Proof pending
Built and public, but the validating artifact hasn't published yet — a target, not a result.
Roadmap / Beta
In beta or on the roadmap. Plan around it; don't buy on it.
Deferred
Not something we claim right now, full stop.
External owner
The evidence lives in another repository; we link it rather than restate it.

// evidence bar

The rules we hold ourselves to.

01 · Exact numbers
Specific counts appear only where a CITE snapshot, parity JSON, or test artifact says the same number.
02 · Partial parity stays partial
GeoServices REST coverage is never described as "100% parity" or "drop-in for everything." Gaps stay tracked in the matrix.
03 · No unconditional cutover
"Clients keep working" gets attached to a parity run for your estate — it isn't asserted in the abstract.
04 · Roadmap labels stay visible
Beta, pilot-scoped, private-beta, and proof-pending labels stay on every page where the underlying claim sits.
05 · External owner = link
If another repo owns the gap, we link the owner ticket rather than restate the work as if it ships from here.
06 · Examples are examples
Terminal sessions, transcripts, and code diffs that are illustrative get labeled "example" — not "live run."
07 · Benchmarks travel with methodology
Performance numbers cite the GeoBench snapshot or stay shape-only with the methodology linked.
08 · Prices are public
The numbers on the pricing page are the numbers. Worked examples show the math, and changes land there first — not in a sales deck.
