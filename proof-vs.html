The honest summary

  • vs. GeoServer: the license models differ: GeoServer is GPL open source; Honua's server is ELv2 source-available and its SDKs are Apache-2.0. In the dated benchmark snapshot, Honua led the listed comparable cells and offers a native, partial GeoServices REST surface plus OpenTelemetry.
  • vs. QGIS Server: QGIS Server has no GeoServices REST surface and no STAC/OData; Honua covers the same OGC surface plus Esri clients on one server. Positioning.
  • Migrating off an Esri estate? The sourced evidence for that decision is the Esri-client compatibility section above, the Compatibility Matrix, and the Migration Evidence page — including the gaps, published honestly.
