// deployment boundaries

Your runtime, your single-tenant deployment.

Honua 2026.1 GA has a single-tenant posture. Multitenancy is available only for Preview/trial evaluation, with no production deployment or GA commitment. Functional, UX and performance work on multitenancy is deferred to 2026.2.

Same server, same protocols
Customer-operated deployments expose the documented GeoServices REST, OGC, OData, gRPC and MCP surfaces within their published release scope. Validate client compatibility against the exact candidate.
Edition and capacity pricing
The published pricing describes customer-operated software licenses and support. It does not offer a hosted or production multi-tenant service.
Baseline secure operation and recovery
Essential secure operation and recoverability are baseline product obligations in every edition. Advanced automation, governance and support are the paid layers around those obligations.
Preview security floor
Cross-tenant disclosure remains a full-severity security defect on Preview surfaces. Preview status never weakens authorization, isolation or audit integrity.
