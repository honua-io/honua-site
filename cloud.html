Honua is software you deploy.
There is no Honua hosted service.

Honua does not offer SaaS, hosting, or a managed service. Honua 2026.1 GA is self-hosted and single-tenant: you select the infrastructure, operate the server, and retain responsibility for your data.

// clear responsibility

Your infrastructure. Your operations and data.

Honua does not operate customer deployments or accept customer production data. Commercial agreements cover self-hosted software and support, not hosting.

2026.1 GA deployment
The GA posture is single-tenant software deployed into infrastructure you control. Production responsibilities, availability, backup, failover, regions, and keys remain with the deploying organization.
Multi-tenancy · Preview / trial only
Any multi-tenant surface is for non-production demo or trial evaluation only. Do not use customer production data. There is no GA, availability, performance, durability, SLO, or scale commitment.
License boundary
The server is source-available under Elastic License 2.0 (ELv2) ↗. Its hosted-service limitation means a third party may not provide Honua to others as a managed service; it does not imply that Honua operates one.
Support is not hosting
Available support and migration services can help your team deploy and operate Honua. They do not transfer infrastructure ownership or operational responsibility to Honua.
