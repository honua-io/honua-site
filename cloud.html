// deployment boundaries

Your runtime, your single-tenant deployment.

2026.1 is not yet released. Its intended GA posture is customer-operated and single-tenant. Multi-tenancy is internal and is not offered for customer deployment. It carries no availability, performance or support claim.

Same server, same protocols
Customer-operated deployments expose the documented GeoServices REST, OGC, OData, gRPC and MCP surfaces within their published release scope. Validate client compatibility against the exact candidate.
Edition and capacity pricing
The edition descriptions cover customer-operated software and optional support. Final rates and metering units are still being developed; no hosted or production multi-tenant service is offered.
Baseline secure operation and recovery
Essential secure operation and recoverability are baseline product obligations in every edition. Advanced automation, governance and support are the paid layers around those obligations.
Isolation floor
Cross-tenant disclosure remains a full-severity security defect. Multi-tenancy is internal and is not a customer deployment. That label never weakens authorization, isolation or audit integrity.
