// what honua cloud is

Everything Honua is. Nothing to operate.

The planned service will expose the documented Honua protocol surfaces and the capabilities included in your selected edition, without asking you to operate the infrastructure. Honua Cloud is a waitlist product today; final packaging and service commitments will be published before general availability.

Same server, same protocols
The planned service will host the documented GeoServices REST, OGC, OData, gRPC, and MCP surfaces. Existing clients still require operation-level compatibility validation before an endpoint move.
Cost scales with workload, not adoption
No per-user fees. No named-user seats. The same capacity-band pricing model as self-hosted — your invoice reflects your workload, not your headcount.
Operated for you
Provisioning, upgrades, backups, health monitoring, and failover — handled. Your team focuses on data and applications, not infrastructure.
Bring your data, keep your exits
PostGIS remains under your control, and standards-based interfaces reduce switching cost. Moving providers can still require endpoint, identity, configuration, and operational changes.
