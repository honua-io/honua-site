Evaluate the release before committing

Honua is preparing the 2026.1 release. Start with the release-candidate overview for release tiers, supported SDK versions and known issues. The release notes record what is available in each version.

Use the capability evidence and client compatibility matrix to choose a pilot scope. Preview and Experimental capabilities keep those labels; availability in a release candidate is not a general-availability commitment. Discuss support and production requirements with us before adopting a production deployment.

Your deployment stays on your infrastructure

A self-hosted deployment keeps your copy of the software and your data on infrastructure you control. If Honua Geo Inc. ceases operating, the applicable license still governs the copy you have. You can retain that copy and maintain it for permitted purposes under its license; continued support, updates and external services are not guaranteed.

Honua Server and Collect use the Fair Core License (FCL-1.0-ALv2). It permits internal use and modification subject to its conditions, including its restrictions on competing use and on changing or circumventing license key functionality. Each version receives an Apache-2.0 license on the second anniversary of its availability.

Read the current Server license and the license included with your version. Other platform components use Apache-2.0; check the relevant repository. Keep copies of the source, dependencies, deployment configuration and backups needed to operate your installation. Commercial support and service commitments require a signed agreement; this page does not promise future service availability.
