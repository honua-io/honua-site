Three steps, and you can stop after any of them.

Each step is small enough to say yes to without a procurement decision. Your current platform stays live throughout.

01 · Show

Send one service URL.

A public map or feature service on ArcGIS Server or GeoServer. We read it the way any client would, import a copy onto our demo server, and send you its address.

02 · Check

Open it in your own tools.

Point your desktop GIS, QGIS, a web map, or a copy of one of your own apps at it. You change one address, and we never need access to your code. On a short call we go through what matched and what did not, and we write both down.

03 · Run beside

One workload, thirty days.

If the copy holds up, we install Honua with you in your own cloud or data centre, and one non-critical workload runs there beside your current server for thirty days, against pass or fail checks we agree in writing first. This is where a whole app moves, with every service it reads.

What each side puts in.

Five places. We keep the group small so every partner works with the people building the product.

From Honua

What you get

  • The install and the migration of your chosen workload, done with you in your environment.
  • A direct line to the people building the product, and a say in what gets built next.
  • No charge during the programme.
  • The price for continuing, in writing, before the thirty days start.

If the checks fail, you have lost a few hours and you keep the written result.

From you

What we ask

  • One named person who owns the workload.
  • A short call each week while it runs.
  • Real clients and real data on a workload that is not critical.
  • Honest written feedback, including what failed.
  • Permission to publish the result if it passes, named or anonymous as you choose.

No purchase commitment at any step.

Who this fits today.

A good first workload

Map and feature services that are published to the public or to staff, read by desktop GIS and web maps that must keep working, on a team that runs ArcGIS Server or GeoServer and can spare an hour a week.

Check client compatibility

Better left for later

Workloads that depend on utility network or parcel fabric editing, on offline field apps, or on a permitting or asset system that names one vendor. Tell us about them anyway, because they shape what we build.
