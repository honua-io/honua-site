INSTALL IN AWS · CLOUDFORMATION + MCP

One CloudFormation stack.
Then your agent takes the wheel.

The AWS Launch Stack is the exact single-architecture container deployment we are qualifying for the AWS Marketplace. There is no Terraform, no build pipeline, no public load balancer, and no Redis cluster to babysit.

1. Launch in your AWS account (~20–30 min): You provide only a stack name, your administrator email (for built-in Amazon Cognito MFA sign-in), and the release-candidate image digest. The template provisions a private VPC, CloudFront with AWS WAF and a private VPC origin, an internal ALB, ECS Fargate tasks, encrypted RDS PostgreSQL 16 with PostGIS, private versioned S3 buckets, same-origin Honua Console (/console), and an independent 1-minute watchdog Lambda.

2. Enable AI via MCP (/mcp) and Amazon Bedrock: Sign in at ConsoleUrl, mint a scoped API key, and connect your personal coding agent (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, or any MCP client) to https://YOUR_HONUA_URL/mcp—or set AiProvider=Bedrock on the stack to run conversational mapmaking in Honua Studio through Amazon Bedrock inside your own AWS account with zero external model API keys. Use your AI agent and @honua/honua-migrate to inventory and import services from your current GIS server, author maps, and port web applications to the Honua SDKs and MapLibre.

3. Operate safely through the Ops Inbox: Agent configuration changes and @sha256 CVE image updates land as reviewable proposals in your Console Ops Inbox (/console/inbox) with preflight checks, human-in-the-loop approval, separation of duties, automatic rollback alarms, and a tamper-evident HMAC-SHA256 policy audit trail.

PRIMARY & SECONDARY TARGET MARKETS · PILOTS & LICENSING

Primary focus: towns & counties.
Plus state/regional open-data clearinghouses, planning, conservation, AEC & insurance.

Small and mid-sized local governments are our primary target market—whether you face an Esri population-band jump, a third-party hosting renewal, the June 2027 Web AppBuilder retirement, or the December 2027 ArcGIS Server 10.9.1 support deadline. Alongside towns and counties, we are actively recruiting pilot partners for Regional & State Open-Data Clearinghouses and across four secondary market segments: Planning Agencies, Conservation & Non-Profits, AEC & Municipal Engineering Firms, and Insurance & Climate Risk.

01 · Opt-in fixed-price migration pilot

Turnkey migration of your services & apps—credited toward your license.

If your team wants Honua to migrate a defined set of map/feature services and rebuild or port a defined set of web apps (parcel viewers, 500-ft abutter notices, 311, public works, open-data portals, or retiring Web AppBuilder apps), you can opt into a fixed-price turnkey pilot contract. We scan your estate up front, quote a fixed cap (sized to fit under local small-purchase thresholds where needed), absorb any overrun, bill only when the migrated services and apps pass written parity checks alongside your live server, and credit the pilot fee toward your annual license fees.

02 · Town & county licensing direction

Pegged to population bands—like Esri's bands, at a fraction of the cost.

Local governments budget one predictable annual line item, not fluctuating per-user seat types or service-credit meters. While final list fees are still being finalized with our pilot cohort, our town and county pricing model follows familiar population bands (Under 25k, 25k–50k, 50k–125k, and 125k+ residents) at a fraction of incumbent enterprise agreement and third-party hosting costs—with unlimited viewers, editors, and published services and multi-year price stability.

03 · Open-data portals & state / regional GIS clearinghouses

Compose portal pages in Studio or via MCP—and auto-publish DCAT, STAC & OGC Records.

Replacing ArcGIS Hub, Socrata / Tyler Data & Insights, CKAN, or an aging GeoNetwork / GeoNode clearinghouse? In Honua, you author discovery metadata once in Console and automatically serve anonymous public dataset pages (/public), DCAT-US 3.0 data.json (for Data.gov and statewide federation), Schema.org Dataset JSON-LD (for Google Dataset Search), OGC API Records, STAC (for statewide imagery and LiDAR), OData v4 (for Excel and Power BI), and CDN-backed GeoParquet, PMTiles, and COG downloads with zero credit burn. Better yet, use Honua Studio or /mcp (honua_studio_* tools) to pull live services, maps, dashboards, Vega-Lite charts, and tables into templated multi-page portal apps and narrative reports (including importing existing ArcGIS StoryMaps, Hub pages, and Dashboards)—with dependency-closure checks and 1-click version rollback.

04 · Secondary market segments

Planning agencies, conservation / non-profits, AEC & insurance.

We have tailored workflows and live Maui showcases for our four secondary segments: Planning Agencies & MPOs (zoning, SMA coastal overlays, 500-ft abutter buffers, infill suitability, regional data clearinghouses), Conservation & Non-Profits (watershed, stream, and sea-level rise stewardship + STAC/COG imagery without per-seat fees), AEC Firms (multi-town portfolio hosting, NBI bridge/culvert inspection, CIP drafting, 3D buildings + terrain), and Insurance & Climate Risk (parcel/building TIV aggregation across FEMA flood AE/VE, tsunami, and SLR rasters). Commercial pricing for non-municipal organizations is being finalized with the beta cohort around deployment capacity and edition—never named-user head count—and is provided in writing before any paid work or 30-day run starts.
