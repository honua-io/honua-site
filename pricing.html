// what changes the price

Capacity sets the band.
Adoption does not.

One visible unit
One serving unit is one production serving node up to 8 vCPU and 32 GiB RAM. The written quote states how larger or unusual node shapes count.
Your routine production footprint
Choose the band that covers normal production scale. Autoscaling inside that band does not change the invoice during the term. Sustained or recurring growth moves to a larger band under the order terms.
No seat or usage meter
Add operators, analysts, applications, requests, and data without a separate Honua fee. More workload affects price only when it requires more production serving capacity.
Non-production is excluded
Development, test, staging, and cold-standby disaster-recovery capacity do not count toward the paid band.
Cloud costs stay separate
The prices above cover the annual Honua software license. Compute, storage, networking, observability, and other cloud-provider charges remain yours.
Short bursts are handled in the contract
Standard terms include short-burst grace above the committed band. Serverless, emergency surge, and unusual deployment shapes require explicit terms in the order.

// license operation & renewal

Plan renewal before expiry.

Community requires no license and is never affected by license expiry. Pro and Enterprise follow a strict contract: a missing, invalid or expired license refuses startup with a non-zero exit. A paid instance never falls back to Community.

Declare Licensing__Edition=Pro or Enterprise on each paid server and worker. At runtime expiry, all licensed operations stop, including reads and exports of existing data. In-flight jobs are cancelled and recorded as failed with reason license expired. Partial outputs are cleaned up or explicitly marked incomplete and are never presented as completed results.

Admin banners and logs warn at 30, 14, 7 and 1 days before expiry. Renew before expiry, or finish the documented backup/export procedure before expiry. Exporting through an expired paid instance is not a recovery path.

A renewed license takes effect on restart or at the running instance's one-minute re-validation interval. An accepted admin upload applies immediately and survives restart. Renew the authoritative source, including any persisted upload override; update every replica and worker. Jobs cancelled at expiry remain failed and must be submitted again after renewal. Health probes and authenticated license recovery operations remain reachable.

Exact Pro startup errors are shown below; Enterprise uses Enterprise in both edition positions:

Honua Pro startup refused: license missing. Install a valid Pro license in the configured licensing source and restart. Community fallback is disabled.
Honua Pro startup refused: license invalid. Install a valid Pro license in the configured licensing source and restart. Community fallback is disabled.
Honua Pro startup refused: license expired. Install a valid Pro license in the configured licensing source and restart. Community fallback is disabled.

Use the supported SDK's getLicenseStatus operation to verify renewal. See the operator troubleshooting guide for each state and remedy. Keep license material and keys out of logs and support comments.
