Elevation Query

Query elevation profile and point-value surfaces. Sun/shadow, slice, line-of-sight, and viewshed analytics are Pro-gated separately.

This is the independent server elevation profile/value analysis surface. Its proving-test count does not qualify a 3D scene renderer or Cesium terrain service.

Counts below are attributed source tests, not a passing execution receipt or exact-candidate certification.
