Less operations toil,
and every change still governed.

Honua gives agents a read-only way to discover and analyze GIS services today. For operations teams, a controlled operator can investigate incidents, plan work, and draft reviewable changes for one environment. Instead of manually correlating product-specific signals, operators get a bounded evidence package and a proposed next step they can accept, revise, or reject. People and policy stay in charge of anything that can alter a system.

01 · Discover and query

Give agents a governed view of GIS data.

The public MCP discovery and query surfaces are available now. Compatible agents can find services, inspect schemas, run filtered queries and statistics, and perform bounded spatial analysis without changing source data. Setup and specification ↗

02 · Operate one environment PILOT ACCESS

Turn an incident into evidence and a reviewable plan.

honua-devops can inspect health, metrics, and errors; explain likely causes; plan a deployment; and draft a GitOps proposal. Planning does not apply changes. A narrowly scoped policy can permit specific deterministic actions, and each tool call produces a redacted structured record.

03 · Fleet automation PARTIAL

Verify and recover are implemented. Fleet promotion is next.

Every autonomous action is verified after the fact and compensated when it fails to converge. An action with no registered verifier is refused rather than executed. Cross-environment fleet promotion is not yet implemented.

The compatible MCP client supplies model reasoning. Honua's credentials, tool permissions, action policy, and approval flow bound what that model can reach. Review the evidence and limits →

Less operational toil, and no blank check.

The operator starts by gathering evidence. Configuration changes go through a validated specification and dry run, then default to a pull request for human review. Explicit policy is required for the narrow action paths that can apply a change. Diagnosis, approval, and execution remain separate steps.

Evidence first

Health, telemetry, and error signals support a diagnosis, ranked remediation steps, and checks your team can review.

Scoped authority

Tool handlers enforce credentials, permissions, and approval rules. Conversation instructions cannot expand them.

Recorded actions

Tool calls emit redacted records. Your deployment supplies durable storage, retention, and any compliance controls.
