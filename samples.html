Read the state before the claim

Data mode and fallback are separate. A page can begin on a live service and later report a fixture replay or a degraded capability; it should never silently turn one into the other.

Live is observed

Public and demo-live lanes identify the source at runtime. Reachability is not inferred from a static badge.

Fixture is deterministic

Fixtures keep a workflow usable and testable, but are labeled replayed or synthetic rather than current.

Degradation is structured

Missing data, auth, or server capability reduces only the affected lane and leaves visible recovery guidance.
